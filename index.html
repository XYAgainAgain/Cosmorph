Cosmorph

A procedural cosmic background engine. Soothing, beautiful deep-space scenes that gradually drift and evolve.

Open Firmament GitHub

Space Math

One integer generates an entire sky. The same seed renders the same scene everywhere, so you can share your celestial creations with others and revel in the majesty of space together.

Slow & Steady

Nebulae billow, expand, and contract naturally, shadows sweep across volumetric space dust, and stars glitter everywhere. The cosmos morph (that's the name, see?) slowly, but quick enough that you'll live to see the outcome.

Lovely on OLED

Everything is smooth and vibrant, no matter how void-black or H-alpha pink your pixels are. Looks best on OLED panels, though, for sure. The larger the screen, the more starscape you'll see.
